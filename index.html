GSTR‑1 / GSTR‑2 CSV GENERATOR
Formats your invoice data for filing
What this does: Takes invoice data you already have — from a file export or typed in manually — and arranges it into the standard GSTR‑1 (outward supply / sales) or GSTR‑2 (inward supply / purchase) column format used for filing. It does not connect to the GST portal or check anything remotely; the CSV it produces is meant to be uploaded to the official GST offline tool or portal yourself.
